Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2026

Readicheck is a personal task-management app. You can use it offline-only, in which case nothing leaves your device. If you choose to sign in, your tasks are stored on our servers so they can sync across your devices. This policy explains exactly what's stored where, and what we never do with it.

If you don't sign in

Your tasks, categories, tabs, subtasks, notes, due dates, settings, and completion history live in your device's local storage. Nothing is transmitted to us. You are anonymous to us.

If you sign in to enable sync

The following is stored on our servers, scoped to your account:

  • Your email address (used as your sign-in identifier)
  • Your password, hashed (we never see or store the plain password)
  • Your tasks, categories, tabs, subtasks, notes, and due dates
  • Your app settings (theme, accent color, sound preference)
  • Standard authentication metadata (sign-in timestamps, IP address of the most recent sign-in), used solely to keep your account secure

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Your account is isolated from every other account; no other user can read or modify your tasks.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not run any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs in the app.
  • We do not profile your behavior or use your task content for any purpose other than showing it back to you.
  • We do not require an account. Sync is opt-in.

Permissions

Readicheck does not request any sensitive Android permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, microphone, camera, files, or other apps. It needs network access only when you're signed in, so it can sync.

Children's privacy

Readicheck is appropriate for general audiences. Sign-in is optional, and if you don't sign in, no information is collected at all.

Crash reports and analytics

The app does not include any crash-reporting or analytics SDKs. The Google Play Store may collect aggregate, anonymous installation and crash statistics on its own. That data is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours, and Readicheck never receives or accesses it.

Data deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Local data: Tap Settings → Clear all data inside the app, or uninstall the app.
  • Synced data: While signed in, tap Settings → Clear all data. This clears both local and server-side copies.
  • Account deletion: While signed in, tap Settings → Delete account. This immediately and permanently removes your account and every synced task on our servers.

Data retention

Synced data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you stop using the app, you can delete your account from Settings at any time; we don't auto-purge inactive accounts.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, the change will be announced inside the app before it takes effect.
