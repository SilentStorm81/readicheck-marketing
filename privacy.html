Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 April 2026

Readicheck is a personal task-management app designed to keep your data on your device. This policy explains what we do, and don't do, with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Readicheck does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, task content, or usage analytics. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers in the app.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Your tasks, categories, tabs, subtasks, notes, and due dates
  • Your app settings (theme, accent color, sound preference)
  • Your completion history (for streaks and the weekly review)

This data is stored using your device's standard local storage. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it through your device's own sharing tools (e.g., copying a task to a messaging app).

Permissions

Readicheck does not request any sensitive Android permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, microphone, camera, files, or other apps.

Children's privacy

Readicheck is appropriate for general audiences. Because we collect no data of any kind, the app complies with COPPA and similar children's privacy laws by design.

Crash reports and analytics

The app does not include any crash-reporting or analytics SDKs. The Google Play Store may collect aggregate, anonymous installation and crash statistics on its own. That data is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours, and Readicheck never receives or accesses it.

Data deletion

Because all your data is local, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Tapping Settings → Clear all data inside the app
  • Uninstalling the app, which removes all stored data

Changes to this policy

If we ever add features that involve collecting data (e.g., optional cloud sync), this page will be updated and the change will be announced inside the app before such features are enabled. Existing data will never be transmitted without your explicit consent.
