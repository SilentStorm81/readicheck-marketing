Delete your data or account

Last updated: 3 May 2026

Readicheck is published on Google Play by ReadiDev. You have two ways to delete data, depending on whether you want to keep your account or remove it entirely.

Delete just your data (keep your account)

Use this if you want to start fresh — wipe every synced task, category, note, and setting from our servers and from this device, but keep your sign-in so you can rebuild without making a new account.

  1. Open the Readicheck app and sign in.
  2. Tap Settings (top right).
  3. Tap Clear all data and confirm.

The wipe runs immediately and propagates through sync to every other device on the same account. Your email and password remain valid; the account itself is untouched.

Delete your entire account (recommended for full removal)

  1. Open the Readicheck app and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (top right).
  3. Scroll to the Sync section.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

The deletion is immediate. Your account record and every synced row in our database are removed in the same transaction. No further action is required.

If you can't access the app

If you've already uninstalled, or otherwise can't sign in to delete in-app, email info@readicheck.app from the address you signed up with. Subject line: Delete my Readicheck account (or Delete my Readicheck data if you only want the data removed). We'll process the request within 7 days and email you confirmation.

What gets deleted

Everything we have on you. Specifically:

  • Your authentication record (email address and hashed password).
  • Every tab, category, task, subtask, and note ever stored on your account.
  • Your app settings (theme, accent colour, sound preference).
  • Authentication metadata (sign-in timestamps, last-seen IP address).

What is kept

Nothing on our servers. Aggregated, anonymous Play Store install and crash statistics that Google collects independently are governed by Google's privacy policy; Readicheck never receives or accesses that data.

What about local data on my device?

Account deletion removes your data from our servers. Anything cached on your device is wiped automatically when you sign out. To remove any remaining local data, tap Settings → Clear all data, or simply uninstall the app.

Retention period

None. Deletion is immediate and unrecoverable. We don't retain account data after deletion for any purpose, marketing or otherwise. If you change your mind later, you'll need to create a new account from scratch.
